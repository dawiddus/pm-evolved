What changes

Classic PM vs AI PM

AreaClassic project managementAI project / product management
ScopeRequirements are known up front and mostly stable.Feasibility is unknown until you test with real data. Scope is a hypothesis.
PlanningWork breakdown, critical path, fixed milestones.Time-boxed experiments with go / pivot / stop gates.
QualityPass/fail acceptance tests.Statistical quality: precision, recall, eval scores, error budgets. Never 100%.
DoneShipped = done.Shipped = start of monitoring. Models drift and degrade.
Key dependencyPeople and code.Data: availability, quality, rights, labeling.
RiskSchedule, budget, scope.Plus bias, hallucination, privacy, security (prompt injection), regulation.
CostMostly fixed build cost.Variable run cost per request (tokens, GPU), which grows with usage.
Competencies

The six competencies you will build

AI literacy

Explain how ML and LLMs work, what they can't do, and why they fail, to engineers and executives alike.

Data judgement

Assess whether data is sufficient, representative, legally usable and labeled well enough to start.

Experiment-led delivery

Run AI work as staged experiments with clear exit criteria instead of a fixed waterfall plan.

Evaluation & metrics

Define offline evals, online business metrics, and guardrails, and link them to each other.

Governance & risk

Classify use cases under the EU AI Act, run impact assessments, set human oversight.

Strategy & economics

Decide build vs buy, model unit economics, and build the business case and team.

AI Governance Manager

Owns AI risk, compliance (EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001) and responsible-AI processes.
